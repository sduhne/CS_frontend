MENLO COACH
===========

TO START (Windows)
  Unpack the whole zip, then double-click "Start Poker Coach.bat" in the unpacked folder.
  It needs Python 3.10 or newer and nothing else: nothing is installed, no internet is used.
  No Python yet: get it from https://www.python.org/downloads/ and tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
  If Windows asks whether to run the file, choose Run.
  Not yet tried on a Windows computer: if it does not start, the black window says why.

WHAT OPENS
  A black window titled Menlo Coach, which must stay open, and a page in your browser with
  that name. Menlo Coach is the program; its files keep "Poker Coach" in their names.
  On the page you type or click what happens at your table, and the coach answers.
  Three modes: Tournament, Spin & Gold (3 or 6 players), All-In or Fold. Press ? on the page for the keys.
  Nothing reads your screen, nothing clicks for you, nothing is sent anywhere.

WHERE YOUR TABLES ARE SAVED
  In this folder, in tournament\app, after every change. The next start goes on from there.

TO STOP
  Close the black window. Closing only the page leaves the coach running: the page comes
  back at the address the black window shows (usually http://127.0.0.1:8765/).
  Starting it a second time opens the page of the copy that is already running.

STUDY AND PLAY MONEY ONLY
  Real-money sites ban real-time help. Using this during a real-money game can close an account.

THE QUICK GUIDE (new)
  Double-click "Open Quick Guide.bat" (or guide\index.html). A page opens in your browser:
  tap your two cards, what happened before you, your seat and your stack in big blinds,
  and it shows the play, why, and the whole chart. It needs no Python and no black window.
  It looks answers up in tables; it reads nothing from your screen.

ALSO HERE
  "Start Screen Reader (old).bat"  the older program that reads the table from the screen.
  "Build exe.bat"   makes dist\PokerCoach.exe, the same table app as one program that runs
                    without Python. It shows a small window with a Stop button, and saves
                    your tables in PokerCoach\app in your user folder.
  Mac or Linux      in a terminal in this folder:  python3 -m poker_coach app
  README.md         everything else: what to change (blinds, stacks and keys are in
                    poker_coach\app\defaults.py), the commands, the tests
